1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how CandleLab ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you download, install, and use the CandleLab mobile application (the "Application") published on Google Play. We are dedicated to maintaining the highest privacy standards and ensuring full compliance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

The Application is built with privacy-by-design principles. We do NOT collect, access, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, postal addresses, phone numbers, or government IDs.
  • No User Accounts or Passwords: The Application requires no sign-up, login, or social authentication.
  • No Brokerage or Banking Credentials: The Application never asks for, handles, or stores trading passwords, broker API keys, wallet seed phrases, credit card details, or bank account information.
  • No Real-Money Financial Transactions: CandleLab does not process deposits, withdrawals, or real-money trade routing.
  • No Location or Biometric Data: We do not track GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi location, coarse/fine location, or biometric data.
  • No Device Sensors or Media Libraries: We do not access your microphone, camera, contacts, or media libraries.

3. Information Handled Strictly on Your Device

The Application handles a limited amount of operational data strictly on your local device:

  • Local On-Device Database Storage: Simulated paper-trading balances ($10,000 demo), backtesting results, custom strategy preferences, and trade logs are stored locally on your device in an encrypted Room/SQLite database. This data is never synchronized with or sent to any remote server.
  • User-Selected CSV Files: When you import a custom historical candlestick CSV file for backtesting, the file is read and parsed locally in device memory via the native Android Document Picker. Imported data is never transmitted across the network or shared with external parties.
  • Public Market Price Feeds: The Application queries public cryptocurrency endpoints (such as public Binance REST API endpoints) over encrypted HTTPS connections solely to fetch public candlestick data (OHLCV). These requests contain no personal information, advertising IDs, or device identifiers.

4. Third-Party Services and Analytics

CandleLab does not employ intrusive third-party ad networks or tracking frameworks:

  • Public Market Endpoints: Network requests for candlestick prices are made directly to public market data servers over secure TLS/HTTPS.
  • Google Play Services: Standard system libraries provided by Google LLC may generate anonymous diagnostic or crash information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Android Permissions

CandleLab requests only the minimum essential permissions necessary for technical operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely to fetch public market price feeds over secure HTTPS.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to check network status before making market price requests.

In accordance with Google Play developer policies, CandleLab does NOT request broad external storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE). File selection is handled using the Android system Document Picker.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

Because all application data is stored locally on your physical device:

  • You retain 100% control over your data at all times.
  • To delete all data, go to: Android Settings → Apps → CandleLab → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.
  • Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all databases, settings, and logs from your device.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

CandleLab is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 18 in jurisdictions where market analytics software requires legal majority). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Financial & Investment Risk Disclaimer

Disclaimer: CandleLab is an educational, statistical analysis, and simulated paper-trading research utility. It does NOT offer financial, investment, or trading advice. Past backtesting performance does not guarantee future results.

9. Security Practices

All external network requests use modern TLS encryption (HTTPS). On-device application data is isolated within the secure Android application sandbox, preventing unauthorized access by other apps.
